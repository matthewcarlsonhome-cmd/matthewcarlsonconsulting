What the score means

Three ranges, three next steps

0–45

Start with the process, not the AI

Something upstream isn't ready: the work isn't written down, the data isn't reachable, or nobody will own the result. An AI Readiness Sprint fixes that order of operations in two weeks and ends with a roadmap.

50–70

Pick one process and pilot it

You're close. Choose the single process with the clearest hours-saved number, run it on a small slice in a test environment, and measure before scaling.

75–100

Build it

The inputs, outputs and owners exist. A build sprint of two to six weeks takes it from spec to a tested system your team runs.
