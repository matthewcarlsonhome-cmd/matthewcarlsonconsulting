# Google Ads Campaign Plan: Matthew Carlson Consulting
## Madison & Wisconsin Market | AI-Native Marketing Agency

**Monthly Budget:** $200 (~$6.67/day)
**Target Market:** Madison, WI & Greater Wisconsin
**Campaign Focus:** High-ROAS keywords for AI automation, workflow automation, and Google Shopping services

---

## Executive Summary

This campaign plan targets the Madison, Wisconsin market with a focus on high-intent, long-tail keywords that maximize ROAS on a $200/month budget. Given industry benchmarks showing professional services at $5-$6.75 CPC, we'll prioritize specific, high-intent terms with lower competition to stretch the budget further.

**Key Strategy Points:**
- Focus on long-tail, high-intent keywords (3+ words) for lower CPC
- Geographic targeting limited to Madison/Dane County with Wisconsin expansion
- Emphasize unique AI-native positioning vs. traditional agencies
- Use Target CPA bidding to maximize conversions on limited budget
