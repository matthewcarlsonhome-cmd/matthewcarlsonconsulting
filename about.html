Our Story

Where Vision Meets Execution

Matthew Carlson Consulting was founded on a simple observation: businesses know what they want to achieve with AI and automation, but the path from concept to deployment is often murky, slow, and expensive.

With over a decade of experience at the intersection of technology and marketing, we bridge that gap. We take complex AI capabilities and translate them into practical, deployable solutions that deliver measurable business results—fast.

Based in Madison, Wisconsin, we work with businesses and marketing agencies who understand the potential of AI but need a partner who can actually build it. From Google Shopping feed automation to custom document retrieval systems, we turn "wouldn't it be great if..." into "here's how it works."
