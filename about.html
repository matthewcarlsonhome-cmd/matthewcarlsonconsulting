About

AI Solutions Engineer. Former $40M marketing operator.

I build production AI systems for marketing teams, agencies and professional-services firms.

Before AI, I spent 11 years at Duluth Trading running a $40M+ digital marketing budget that drove $200M+ in annual revenue, owning analytics, attribution, paid media and data feeds. That's why every system I build is scoped against hours saved, revenue and risk, and why it ships with tests and a cost model instead of a demo.

How I work

Principles that came from shipping, not slides

Gate before you scale

One graded sample and a consistency check cost hours. Regenerating dozens of outputs from a bad source costs days.

Confidence is a field

Every AI-researched fact is labeled verified, derived or needs input, so people know what they can trust.

Verify the agent's boundary

Autonomous builds run only in named test environments, with location checks and API readback every run.

Report where the work happens

Alerts land as tasks in the tool people already use, not in another dashboard they have to remember.

Test suites make demos safe

A voice agent meets a client only after it passes its test cases and the safety regression.

Price build and run separately

Launch, build, maintenance and usage are separate lines, so nobody is surprised later.

Let's talk about what you want AI to run.

A 30-minute call is usually enough to know whether it's worth building.
